# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it kindly.
- Focus on what is best for the community and users.
- Protect privacy: never post anyone's financial data or credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainer through a
[GitHub private security advisory](https://github.com/deepakdaneva/arthapath/security/advisories/new)
marked "Code of Conduct", or by contacting the maintainer via their GitHub
profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily
or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful, following
the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
