Your key is used directly from this device to call the provider, so anything running on this device (for example a malicious browser extension) could read it while ArthaPath is unlocked. When you chat, your message and relevant financial context are sent from this device to the provider in use. How that provider stores or uses them depends on the provider and your key's plan, under the provider's terms; ArthaPath does not control or receive that data. Identity numbers and secrets that ArthaPath recognizes are removed before anything is saved or sent.
