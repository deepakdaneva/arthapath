Privacy and encryption

Only you can read your finances

  • Data is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) with a key derived from your private PIN. The database stores only ciphertext.
  • Your PIN never leaves your device. If you forget it, no one can recover your data.
  • When you use AI features, your question and the relevant financial context are sent to the AI provider you configured, under that provider's terms.
  • Every number is calculated by tested code, not guessed by AI.
