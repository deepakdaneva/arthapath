# Support

- **Questions and ideas:** open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/deepakdaneva/arthapath/discussions)
  or an issue using the *Question* template.
- **Bugs:** use the *Bug report* issue template. Never paste real financial
  data, API keys, or your encryption PIN into an issue.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do not open a public issue. Follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

ArthaPath is a community open-source project maintained on a best-effort
basis. There is no paid support and no service-level guarantee.

**Lost encryption PIN:** ArthaPath cannot recover your data. The PIN never
leaves your device and there is no backdoor. See
[docs/architecture/ENCRYPTION_ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/architecture/ENCRYPTION_ARCHITECTURE.md).
